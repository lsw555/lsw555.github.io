About Sanguk Lee.

I am an Assistant Professor in the Division of Media & Communication at Hankuk University of Foreign Studies.

My work brings together computational social science, science communication, and the study of emerging media. I am interested in how data, algorithms, and media systems shape people’s beliefs and everyday communication.

Before joining HUFS, I was a Postdoctoral Associate at Yale University’s School of the Environment and an Assistant Professor at Texas Christian University. I received my Ph.D. in Communication from Michigan State University.

My methodological toolkit includes natural language processing, machine learning, network analysis, spatial and time-series analysis, web scraping, and Python, R, and SQL.
