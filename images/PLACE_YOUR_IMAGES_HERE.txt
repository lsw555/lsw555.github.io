Place the following images in this folder:

1. profile.jpg - Your profile/headshot photo
   Recommended size: 500x500px (square)

2. project1.jpg - Screenshot or image of your first project
   Recommended size: 800x600px

3. project2.jpg - Screenshot or image of your second project
   Recommended size: 800x600px

4. project3.jpg - Screenshot or image of your third project
   Recommended size: 800x600px

Tips:
- Use JPG format for photos
- Keep file sizes under 500KB for faster loading
- You can compress images at: tinypng.com
- Use descriptive filenames if you add more images
