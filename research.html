Research

Computational methods for consequential communication questions

I combine communication theory with large-scale data to understand how people encounter, interpret, and respond to changing information environments.

01

Climate change communication

I study climate belief systems, persuasive communication, public opinion, and the role of news media in shaping how people make sense of climate change.

02

AI, algorithms, and society

I investigate large language models, algorithmic bias, and changing public discourse around artificial intelligence and its social consequences.

03

Networked communication

I use network approaches to examine online support, belief systems, misinformation, and the social structures that shape communication.
